# Business & Marketing Prompts (10)

## 1. Business Plan Outline
Create a business plan outline for [BUSINESS IDEA].
Sections: Executive summary, problem, solution, market, competition, revenue model, costs, team, milestones.

## 2. Competitor Analysis
Analyze competitors for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] in [MARKET].
For each competitor: strengths, weaknesses, pricing, target audience, differentiators.

## 3. Customer Persona
Create a customer persona for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].
Demographic, psychographic, pain points, goals, buying behavior, preferred channels.

## 4. SWOT Analysis
Perform a SWOT analysis for [COMPANY/PROJECT].
Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. Include specific examples.

## 5. Marketing Strategy
Create a marketing strategy for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE].
Channels, messaging, budget allocation, KPIs, timeline.

## 6. Pricing Strategy
Recommend a pricing strategy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].
Consider: value proposition, competitor pricing, customer willingness to pay, margins.

## 7. Pitch Deck Outline
Create a pitch deck outline for [STARTUP/BUSINESS].
10-12 slides. Each slide: title, key points, visual suggestion.

## 8. Customer Survey Questions
Generate 10 survey questions to understand [RESEARCH GOAL].
Mix of multiple choice, scale, and open-ended. Avoid bias.

## 9. Sales Email
Write a cold sales email for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] to [PROSPECT ROLE].
Subject line (3 options). Personalization placeholder. Value proposition. CTA.

## 10. Meeting Agenda
Create a meeting agenda for [MEETING TYPE] with [PARTICIPANTS].
Duration: [LENGTH]. Objectives. Agenda items with time allocations.
